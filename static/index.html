Turn your photos into 3D printable parts — $2 filament instead of $20 plastic

How It Works

1. Place your object on college-ruled paper (lines are 7.1mm apart)

2. Take a photo from directly above

3. Upload it here — we'll detect the object, measure it, and generate printable STL files

4. Download your custom parts and print them!

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • Use college-ruled paper (standard notebook paper with blue lines)
  • Take photo from directly above (not at an angle)
  • Ensure good lighting and contrast
  • Works best for: hooks, brackets, holders, clips, knobs, baskets, spacers
  • For complex objects, try multiple angles and pick the best result
